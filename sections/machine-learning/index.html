Open Questions

  • Does long context obviate retrieval, or relocate it? amans-ai-journal-primers-retrieval-augmented treats this as unresolved: longer windows change what needs retrieving and move the cost without removing it.
  • Why does RLHF beat SFT? rlhf-reinforcement-learning-from-human-feedbackrlhf offers three hypotheses (diversity, negative feedback, hallucination) and, in its own words, no foolproof argument for any of them.
  • Does architectural design contradict the bitter lesson? Humans designed the Transformer, which delivered the largest recent gain. scaling-beats-knowledge-engineering offers a reconciliation: it encodes how to use compute, not what is true about language. This claim remains unsettled and worth testing against the next architecture.
  • Can an agent revise a plan after an unexpected error? llm-powered-autonomous-agents-lillog names this as an open limitation; amans-ai-journal-primers-agents adds protocols and frameworks around it without resolving it.
