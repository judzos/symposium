Philosophy & Meaning

“The question is not what is the meaning of life — that’s unanswerable. The question is how you live knowing there may not be a cosmic answer.” — ixcarus

The existential layer of the corpus. These essays sit underneath all the neuroscience and psychology — they address the questions that frameworks cannot answer: Why does any of this matter? Who is the “I” doing all this improvement? What do we do with the certainty that we will die?

ixcarus does not resolve these questions. He engages them honestly — which is rarer.

The Meaning Question

Sisyphus and the Absurd

  • Sisyphus-Myth (Camus) — condemned to roll a boulder uphill forever; the archetypal image of meaningless repetitive effort
  • Camus’s answer: “we must imagine Sisyphus happy” — the struggle itself is enough to fill a man’s heart; meaning is made in the act, not found in the outcome
  • The absurd = the gap between the human need for meaning and the universe’s silence on the matter
  • Living in the absurd: not pretending the gap doesn’t exist (religion), not collapsing into it (nihilism), but holding the tension and acting anyway

What is "the absurd," in Camus's sense?

The gap between the human need for meaning and the universe’s silence about it. Living in it means neither denying the gap nor collapsing into it, but holding the tension and acting anyway.

Terror Management Theory

  • Terror-Management-Theory — awareness of mortality drives most human behavior; we build systems of meaning, achievement, legacy, and cultural worldview to transcend biological insignificance
  • Every human meaning-making project (religion, politics, career, family) can be understood as an attempt to symbolically outlive the body
  • Anxiety about death is the engine beneath most striving — including striving for self-improvement

What does Terror Management Theory claim is beneath most striving?

Mortality awareness. Achievement, legacy, status and worldview commitment are read as attempts to symbolically outlive the body — self-improvement included.

Meaning as Construction

  • Meaning-Construction — Viktor Frankl: meaning is not found, it is made; through work, love, and how we face unavoidable suffering
  • Neuroscience angle: purpose activates reward circuits; people with strong sense of purpose live longer, have lower cortisol, better immune function — meaning is physically beneficial
  • The post reflects genuine personal wrestling with the question — ixcarus does not offer a resolved answer; he offers honest engagement with the uncertainty

On Frankl's account, is meaning found or made?

Made — through work, through love, and through how unavoidable suffering is faced. It is constructed rather than discovered, which is why it survives the absence of a cosmic answer.

Consciousness: The Hard Problem

Why It’s Unsolved

  • Hard-Problem-of-Consciousness (David Chalmers) — science can explain neural correlates of experience but cannot explain why there is subjective experience — the “what it’s like” — at all
  • All theories (Global Workspace Theory, IIT, predictive processing, panpsychism) explain correlates of consciousness but not the origin of subjective experience
  • The “I’m aware that I’m aware” loop — meta-cognition points to something the brain cannot explain about itself

The Practical Implication

  • If “you” cannot be located in the brain — if consciousness emerges rather than residing in a fixed place — then you are not stuck as the current version
  • The self is process, not substance; it is what the system is doing, not what the system is
  • This is either the most terrifying or most liberating realization available to a human being

What follows practically from the self being process rather than substance?

That you are not stuck as the current version. If consciousness emerges from what the system is doing rather than residing somewhere fixed, there is nothing fixed to be stuck as.

Belief Systems: The Universal Structure

Nobody Is Without Faith

  • Belief-Systems — even secular people have rigid unprovable beliefs: about success, relationships, self-worth, possibility — and structure behavior around them as strictly as doctrine
  • Belief provides: predictability (reduces cognitive load) · meaning (gives purpose) · tribal membership (in-group identity) · anxiety management (terror management)
  • Neuroscience of belief: beliefs become neural patterns through repetition — challenging a core belief triggers the same threat response as physical danger (literally painful)
  • Belief update resistance: the stronger the identity investment in a belief, the more evidence required to change it — confirmation bias as survival mechanism

Why does challenging someone's core belief feel like an attack on them?

Beliefs become neural patterns through repetition, and challenging one triggers the same threat response as physical danger. The defensiveness is a threat reaction, not stubbornness.

The Practical Question

The question is not whether you have beliefs (you do) — it is whether you have examined which ones are serving you and which are limiting you.

Why does ixcarus say secular people are not without faith?

Because they hold rigid unprovable beliefs too — about success, worth, relationships and possibility — and structure behaviour around them as strictly as any doctrine.

Radical Optimism and Creative Potential

You Are a God (Literally)

  • Every physical object around you was once only a thought — this reveals the extraordinary nature of human creative potential
  • The creative faculty is god-like: the ability to conceive something in the mind and bring it into physical existence separates humans from all other species
  • Most people have suppressed this faculty by absorbing “be realistic” conditioning — instruction designed to prevent competition, not to serve the recipient
  • The “burning feeling of being meant for more” is not delusion — it is the signal of untapped creative faculty pushing toward expression

→ Source: you-are-a-god

Radical Optimism

  • Radical-Optimism — the power of believing you can create the thing before having evidence you can
  • This is not naive positivity — it is a functional strategy; belief in the outcome activates the dopaminergic pursuit system and primes neuroplasticity for the learning required
  • The placebo effect (placebo-effect in Consciousness-and-Identity) proves belief alone generates real physiological change — this principle extends to ambition

→ Source: radical-optimism-delusion

Japanese Philosophy as Applied Meaning

The Japanese philosophical concepts ixcarus draws on are practical meaning-frameworks. See also: Japanese-Philosophy

  • Ikigai — purpose at the intersection of (1) what you love, (2) what you’re good at, (3) what the world needs, (4) what you can be paid for; the convergence point is the reason for being
  • Wabi-Sabi — beauty of incompleteness; the imperfect start is more meaningful than the perfect plan that never starts; embracing the unfinished is maturity
  • Kintsugi — broken things repaired with gold; scars made visible rather than hidden; the wound is part of the story, not a defect to conceal
  • Shu-Ha-Ri — follow the rule, break the rule, become the rule; the stages of mastery that all transformation passes through

What are the three stages of Shu-Ha-Ri?

Follow the rule, break the rule, become the rule. Mastery passes through obedience and departure before the rule stops being something external at all.

These are not aesthetics — they are operational philosophies for navigating change, failure, and meaning.

Synchronicity and Underlying Order

  • Synchronicity (Jung) — coincidences experienced as meaningfully connected despite no known causal pathway
  • ixcarus’s position: whether or not there is actual cosmic design, acting as if there is one produces different behavior (attention, action, openness) than acting as if there isn’t
  • Subjective meaning-making: the value of synchronicity thinking is not epistemological but functional — it opens you to patterns you would otherwise dismiss

What is ixcarus's position on synchronicity?

That its value is functional rather than epistemological. Whether or not there is cosmic design, acting as though there is changes attention, action and openness — and that change is real.

  • Perception and direction: correspondence and attraction become selective attention plus consistent behavior; inspired action becomes identity congruence.
