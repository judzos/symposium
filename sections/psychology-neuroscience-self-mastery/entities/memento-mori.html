Memento Mori

Remember that you will die — not to induce dread but because mortality genuinely felt, rather than intellectually acknowledged, is one of the most clarifying instruments available for making decisions.

The Practice

The Stoic formulation, paired in the source with the Japanese mono no aware — the bittersweet awareness of impermanence, which “produces not despair but a heightened appreciation for the present moment precisely because it will not last.”

The intent is stated explicitly: “these weren’t philosophies built to depress people and cause doom and gloom. They are tools for waking people up.”

What does mono no aware add to the Stoic formulation?

That impermanence heightens rather than diminishes. Awareness that a moment will not last produces sharper appreciation of it, not despair.

The Freedom in Being Forgotten

Nobody can name who invented the stop sign — an invention used millions of times daily worldwide, its creator “entirely absorbed by time.” Most people cannot name their own great-great-grandparents.

Wanting desperately to be remembered “is your ego speaking… the part of you that hasn’t made peace with the reality that this is temporary.” But the same oblivion releases: nobody will remember your failures either — “the moment you hesitated, the risk you didn’t take, the embarrassment you were afraid of, the opinion you worried too much about.”

How is being forgotten a release rather than only a loss?

The same oblivion covers the failures. Nobody will remember the embarrassment or the opinion you agonised over either — which removes most of what the fear was protecting.

The Three Substitutions

The practical instrument, applying scale to stuck decisions:

  • The thing you fear starting — “put it next to 75 summers and tell me the risk of trying is worse than the certainty of not.”
  • The opinion you have been worried about — put it next to nobody knowing any of your names in 200 years.
  • The comfort chosen over growth — put it against “the particular regret of a person who arrives at the end of their run knowing they played it safe.”

How does the substitution technique dissolve a stuck decision?

By changing the comparison. Set against 75 summers, the risk of trying is weighed against the certainty of not — a comparison the fear was avoiding by keeping the frame small.

Existing Versus Living

Existing is biological: heart beating, lungs working, time passing. Living is “being present enough to feel the specific texture of a moment while it’s happening rather than when you’re looking back at it.” The mechanism keeping most people in the first is Comfort-Zone — comfort requires no growth, novelty, risk or stretch, and therefore eliminates the exact conditions producing a life worth having.

What separates existing from living, on this account?

When the moment is felt. Existing is the biology continuing; living is being present enough to feel the texture of a moment while it happens rather than in retrospect.
